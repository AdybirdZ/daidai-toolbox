Chinese traditional star-name and HIP-identifier mappings in this internal application are adapted from the Chinese Sky Culture in Stellarium, commit 568d88df8126c45949e19564fe312de577340e5c. The sky-culture data is marked CC BY-SA 4.0. This application selects 20 entries and adapts their representation for its local chart. License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . This package contains only the listed numerical facts, not Stellarium code, its binary star catalogue, or images. See docs/ASTRONOMY-3.0.4.md and docs/ASTRONOMY-3.0.4.json for the entries and changes.
